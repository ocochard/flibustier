# The ships of the open pack

The game has nine ship classes. `ship.py` builds each one in Blender
from the measures below, in metres, and renders it:
- sixteen headings for sailing;
- the side view, from whole to sinking, for the sea battle's damage
  picture.

The measures come from the research of 2026-10-27. Values marked
**[E]** are estimates, from the period rules or from plans, and not
from a source. As a rule, sources give the hull length, beam and gun
counts, but not mast positions, castle heights or bowsprit angles.

## Rules for every class

These are the period's rules of thumb and the ships we can measure, all
**[E]** unless a source is named.

- **Masts along the hull**, as a fraction of its length from the stern:
  - three-masters: mizzen 0.20 to 0.25, main 0.50 to 0.55, fore 0.85
    to 0.88;
  - two-masters: main 0.45, fore 0.80.
- **Mast heights:**
  - The lower mainmast is 2.4 times the beam (1627), the topmast half
    the lower mast and the topgallant half the topmast (Anderson,
    secondhand).
  - The foremast is 0.8 to 0.9 times the main; the mizzen 0.6 to 0.75
    (Vasa: 39.4 / 52.4 = 0.75, VASAFAQ).
  - As a check against measured ships, the mainmast's top above the
    waterline is about 0.85 to 1.0 times the length for big ships, and
    1.1 to 1.2 times for small craft (GH73, KN, AND, VASA).
- **Bowsprit:** raked 30 to 40 degrees above the horizontal before 1650
  and 25 to 35 after. It reaches 0.30 to 0.40 of the length beyond the
  stem.
- **Sprit sails:** a spritsail under the bowsprit on every square
  rig. A spritsail topmast at its end on 17th-century ships (Vasa,
  AND).
- **Castles:** a deck level is 2.0 to 2.2 metres.

## The classes

Each class below gives its proxies, its measures, its features, its
guns and the look the redraw asks for.

### Pinnace

- **Proxies:** Duyfken (1601), whose replica is in DUY, and Kalmar
  Nyckel (KN).
- **Measures:**
  - Duyfken: stem to sternpost 19.94 m, beam 6.01 m, stern 5.5 m above
    the water (DUY). Kalmar Nyckel: 28 m on deck, beam 7.6 m, mast 32 m
    (KN).
  - The model: length 22 m, beam 6.2 m, freeboard 1.8 m **[E]**.
  - A low forecastle; a raised quarterdeck and small poop, about 3 m
    over 0.30 of the length **[E]**.
- **Rig:** three masts (DUY). Course and topsail on the fore and main, a
  lateen mizzen, and a spritsail (DUY). The mainmast stands about
  1.05 × L above the water **[E]**.
- **Features:** lightly built, single-decked, with a square stern (FRP),
  a flat transom and a short beakhead.
- **Guns:** 5 to 16 on one deck (FRP).
- **Look:** a small, slim Dutch three-masted pinnace in dark oiled oak.
  A low single-deck hull with a modest raised stern and a flat, square
  transom. A short beakhead. Square course and topsail on the fore and
  main, a lateen mizzen, a spritsail. A few gun ports.

### Sloop

- **Proxy:** the Bermuda and Jamaica sloops of the later 1600s (BS).
- **Measures:**
  - Up to 21 m (BS). The model: 18 m long, 5.5 m beam, 1.2 m freeboard
    **[E]**.
  - A flush deck with low bulwarks and no castles **[E]**.
- **Rig:**
  - One mast, raked about 10 degrees aft, at 0.62 of the length from
    the stern. It stands about 1.2 × L above the water **[E]**: "high,
    raked masts and long bowsprits and booms" (BS).
  - A gaff mainsail on a long boom, two jibs and a small square topsail
    high on the mast (BS).
  - A long bowsprit, nearly level, 0.55 × L **[E]**.
- **Features:** a low, sleek cedar hull, a transom stern and no
  beakhead.
- **Guns:** 4 to 10 small guns on the open deck **[E]**.
- **Look:** a low, sleek single-masted Caribbean sloop with a flush deck
  and a small square transom. One tall mast raked aft, with a large gaff
  mainsail on a long boom, two jibs on a long, nearly level bowsprit and
  a small square topsail. A bare cedar hull with a black wale and a
  handful of small cannon along the rail.

### Barque

- **Proxy:** the Elizabethan bark, "around 50 or 60 tons, three masts,
  fore and main square-rigged, mizzen fore-and-aft" (NPS).
- **Measures:**
  - The Elizabeth II replica is about 21 m long with a 5.2 m beam
    (secondhand). The model: 21 m × 5.2 m, freeboard 1.5 m **[E]**.
  - A forecastle of one level over 0.15 × L; a quarterdeck and poop of
    about 3.5 m over 0.30 × L **[E]**.
- **Rig:** course and topsail on the fore and main; a lateen mizzen with
  no topsail; a spritsail. The mainmast stands about 1.0 × L above the
  water **[E]**.
- **Features:** a plain merchant-style flat transom, a small beakhead,
  a tarred or brown hull and little gilding.
- **Guns:** 4 to 10 on the upper deck **[E]**.
- **Look:** a small three-masted Elizabethan bark with a stout, plain
  hull, a modest forecastle and a raised square stern. Square course and
  topsail on the fore and main, a lateen mizzen, a spritsail. A tarred
  brown hull with a few red gun-port lids.

### Fluyt (the game's "cargo ship")

- **Measures:**
  - Witsen's standard fluyt (1671) is 37 m, with a length-to-beam ratio
    of 4 to 6 (secondhand). Some are smaller, about 24 m.
  - The model: 35 m long, 8 m beam, freeboard 1.8 m **[E]**, with a
    shallow draft (FL).
  - A "pear-shaped" section (FL): strong tumblehome, the deck 0.65 of
    the beam **[E]**.
  - Hardly any forecastle; a quarterdeck and short poop, about 3 m over
    0.25 × L **[E]**.
- **Rig:** three masts (FL). Course, topsail and topgallant on the fore
  and main **[E]**, the mizzen "often" lateen (FL), and a spritsail. The
  mainmast stands about 0.9 × L above the water **[E]**.
- **Features:** a rounded stern rising to a tall, narrow, flat transom
  **[E]**. Light decoration.
- **Guns:** "minimized or completely eliminated" (FL), 0 to 12 **[E]**.
- **Look:** a Dutch fluyt of the 1650s with a long, narrow cargo hull,
  its sides curving sharply inward above the water. A rounded stern
  topped by a tall, narrow, flat transom, and almost no forecastle.
  Three tall masts: square sails on the fore and main, a lateen mizzen,
  a spritsail. A dark varnished hull with green and ochre trim and few
  gun ports.

### Merchantman

- **Proxies:** Mayflower (1620), Mayflower II and Batavia.
- **Measures:**
  - Mayflower is about 24 to 27 m on deck with a 7.5 m beam (MF);
    Mayflower II is 27 m in the hull with a 7.77 m beam (MF2).
  - The model: 27 m × 7.7 m, freeboard 2.2 m **[E]**.
  - A "30-foot-high, square aft-castle" (MF), about two levels, 4 m
    above the deck over 0.32 × L. A forecastle of 2 m over 0.18 × L
    **[E]**.
- **Rig:** three masts and a spritsail, six sails in all (MF, MF2).
  Course and topsail on the fore and main, a lateen mizzen. The mainmast
  is about as tall as the deck is long **[E]**.
- **Features:** a square tuck stern, modest carving, a beakhead, a buff
  or ochre and black hull.
- **Guns:** Mayflower 4 to 12 light pieces (MF); one gun deck.
- **Look:** a sturdy English merchant ship of about 1620. A broad, deep
  hull with a tall, boxy, square sterncastle two decks high and a
  smaller forecastle. Square course and topsail on the fore and main, a
  lateen mizzen, a spritsail under the steeply raked bowsprit.
  Weathered ochre and black paint, simple red trim and a few small gun
  ports.

### Frigate

This is the 17th-century light frigate, a Dutch or English one.

- **Measures:**
  - Constant Warwick (1645): keel 25.9 m, beam 8.1 m (CW), about 33 m
    on its gun deck **[E]**. A Dutch frigate of 1656 is about 34 m ×
    8.2 m with 36 guns (secondhand).
  - The model: 34 m × 8.2 m, freeboard 2.2 m **[E]**.
  - Low castles, the type's defining look (FRIG): a short forecastle
    and a quarterdeck and small poop of 2.5 m over 0.30 × L **[E]**.
- **Rig:**
  - Course, topsail and topgallant on the fore and main; a lateen mizzen
    under a square mizzen topsail.
  - A spritsail and a spritsail topsail (Anderson, normal for
    1640-1680) **[E]**.
  - The mainmast stands about 1.0 × L above the water **[E]**.
- **Features:** a low hull of "sharpness and fine lines" (CW), a flat
  transom with a modest gallery, a short beakhead, black and ochre bands
  and light gilding.
- **Guns:** 24 to 46. Constant Warwick has 20 on the lower deck, 18 on
  the upper and 4 on the quarterdeck (CW).
- **Look:** a sleek Dutch light frigate of about 1665. A low-slung,
  fine-lined hull with one continuous row of gun ports and a short
  forecastle. A modest raised quarterdeck ending in a flat, carved
  transom. Square courses, topsails and topgallants; a lateen mizzen
  under a small square topsail; a spritsail and spritsail topsail. A
  black hull with ochre bands and red gun-port lids.

### War galleon

- **Proxies:** Vasa (1628), the Spanish war galleon, Prins Willem.
- **Measures:**
  - Vasa: 47.5 m between perpendiculars, 11.7 m beam, draught 4.8 m
    (VASA). The model: 48 m × 11.7 m, freeboard 4.5 m **[E]**.
  - A forecastle of 3 m over 0.18 × L; a quarterdeck, poop and
    half-deck of about 7 m over 0.33 × L **[E]**. Vasa's stern is very
    high (VASA).
- **Rig:**
  - Vasa's masts above the keel: fore 47.0 m, main 52.4 m, mizzen
    39.4 m (VASAFAQ). The main is about 0.9 × L above the water.
  - Course, topsail and topgallant on the fore and main; a lateen mizzen
    and mizzen topsail; a spritsail and spritsail topsail. Vasa carries
    10 sails (VASA).
- **Features:** a long beakhead with a lion figurehead; a tall flat
  transom with galleries; red upper works with gilded and painted
  sculpture (VASA); strong tumblehome.
- **Guns:** Vasa 64, on two full gun decks (VASA).
- **Look:** a massive war galleon of the 1620s. Two full rows of
  red-lidded gun ports. A heavily carved sterncastle several decks high
  with galleries, deep red with gilded and painted sculptures. A long,
  ornate beakhead with a lion figurehead. Square courses, topsails and
  topgallants; a lateen mizzen with a small topsail; two square sails
  on the raked bowsprit.

### Galleon

This is the trading or treasure galleon.

- **Proxy:** the Andalucía replica: 49.4 m overall, 10.1 m beam, draught
  3.0 m, rig 36.9 m tall (AND). Spanish rule: length 3.5 × beam (ORD).
- **Measures:**
  - The model: 40 m on deck × 10 m, freeboard 3.0 m **[E]**.
  - A forecastle of 3 m over 0.18 × L. A "prominent squared off raised
    stern" (GAL) of about 5 m over 0.35 × L **[E]**.
- **Rig:**
  - The fore and main are "noticeably taller" than the mizzen (GAL).
  - Course and topsail on the fore and main, a lateen mizzen, and a
    spritsail and spritsail topsail (AND).
  - No topgallants, as on Andalucía.
- **Features:** a long, low beakhead; square quarter galleries (GAL); a
  flat transom with painted saints or arms; ochre, red and black paint
  with some gilding.
- **Guns:** 20 to 40 **[E]**, on one or two decks.
- **Look:** a Spanish treasure galleon of about 1600. A broad hull with
  a high, square, flat sterncastle three decks tall, painted saints and
  quarter galleries. A lower forecastle and a long, low beakhead. Square
  courses and topsails on the fore and main, a lateen mizzen, two square
  sails on the bowsprit. An ochre and dark red hull with gilded trim and
  one or two rows of gun ports.

### Fast galleon

This is the race-built galleon.

- **Proxies:** Revenge (1577), the Golden Hind.
- **Measures:**
  - Revenge: 28 m on its gun deck, 9.8 m beam (REV). The sources
    disagree on whether 92 feet is the keel.
  - The Golden Hind: 31 m on deck, 6.1 m beam (GH). Its replica:
    31.1 m, mainmast 28 m (GH73).
  - The model: 31 m × 7 m, freeboard 2.5 m **[E]**.
  - "Raced", cut-down castles (RBG): a forecastle of 1.5 m over
    0.15 × L and a stern of 3.5 m over 0.30 × L **[E]**.
- **Rig:** course and topsail on the fore and main, a topgallant on the
  main; a lateen mizzen; a spritsail. The replica carries six sails
  (GH73).
- **Features:** a low, sleek hull, "head of a cod, tail of a mackerel"
  (RBG); a square tuck stern with a gallery; a short beakhead. The
  replica is painted in red, gold and black bands **[E]**.
- **Guns:** Revenge 46 (REV); the Golden Hind 22 (GH73). One full gun
  deck.
- **Look:** a sleek English race-built galleon of the 1580s. A low,
  long hull with cut-down castles: a small forecastle and a moderately
  raised stern with a square transom and a small gallery. One row of
  gun ports. Square courses and topsails on the fore and main, a lateen
  mizzen, a spritsail. The hull painted in bold red, gold and black
  bands.

## What the game shows of them

- **Sailing:** the game draws three sizes of ship (`spr/ship0` to `2`).
  The model uses:
  - the pinnace for the small ships (pinnace, sloop, barque);
  - the merchantman for the medium ones (fluyt, merchantman, frigate);
  - the galleon for the large ones (war galleon, galleon, fast galleon).

  They are drawn at one scale, so a galleon is larger than a pinnace.
- **Sea battle panel:** every class is drawn from the side.
  - **Scale:** at true scale a sloop would be a speck beside a war
    galleon, so each picture's width grows with the square root of the
    ship's length.
  - **Damage:** five steps. The topmasts and the mizzen go, then the
    mainmast falls, then the ship sinks with only stumps left.
